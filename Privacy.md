Privacy Policy for Mallow Bot 

1. Data Collection and Storage

Mallow does not collect, log, store, or sell any personal data, user profiles, or chat history.

2. Message Content

To function correctly, Mallow reads messages in real-time to detect specific commands (like !clear) and trigger words (like greetings or the bot's name). Once a message is processed by the code, it is immediately forgotten. No messages are ever saved to a database or text file.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Because Mallow does not store any data, absolutely no user information is ever shared with or sold to third parties.
